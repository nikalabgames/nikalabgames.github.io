Privacy Policy for Application

This privacy policy applies to the Application for mobile devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

We believe in your right to privacy. The Application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No account creation is required.

Sensors and Permissions

The Application requests access to your device's Gyroscope and Accelerometer. This data is processed locally in real-time solely to provide the 360-degree VR experience. This data is never saved, uploaded, or shared.

Third-Party Services

This Application does not use any third-party services (such as analytics or advertising networks) that collect information used to identify you.

Third-Party Services

The Application may use third-party services that collect information to improve user experience. These include:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: To track app stability and performance.
  • Google AdMob: To display relevant advertisements.

These providers may collect data such as Cookies, Usage Data, and Advertising Identifiers (IDFA/IDG).

Children's Privacy

The Application is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
